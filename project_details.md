## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What services does XFLEX offer?
XFLEX offers a comprehensive range of professional services including Taxation Services (VAT & Corporate Tax), Auditing and Assurance, ESR & UBO Compliance, Administrative and Business Consultancy, and Accounting & Bookkeeping.

### 2. Do you help with VAT registration and filing in the UAE?
Yes, we provide end-to-end VAT services, including registration, deregistration, return preparation, and filing. We also represent clients before the Federal Tax Authority (FTA) to ensure smooth compliance.

### 3. Can you manage my company's bookkeeping and payroll?
Absolutely. We offer monthly bookkeeping, payroll processing, WPS compliance, financial reporting, and reconciliation to ensure your records are accurate and audit-ready.

### 4. Are your auditing services approved for official and regulatory use?
Yes, our auditing services are conducted in accordance with international standards and are suitable for statutory requirements, free zone compliance, and licensing renewals in the UAE.

### 5. What’s the difference between your pricing plans?
Our plans are designed for different business stages: **Essential Compliance** covers basic tax setup; **Growth & Monitoring** handles ongoing monthly filings; and **Tax Protection & Advisory** provides full-scale risk management and advisory support.

### 6. Do you help with company setup in the UAE?
Yes, we specialize in business setup and licensing across UAE Mainland, Free Zones, and Offshore jurisdictions, providing all necessary consultancy and PRO services.

### 7. What is ESR and UBO compliance, and do I need it?
ESR (Economic Substance Regulations) and UBO (Ultimate Beneficial Ownership) are mandatory regulatory frameworks in the UAE. Most businesses need to assess their status and file notifications or reports; we provide full support for these filings.

### 8. Do you offer one-time services or only monthly subscriptions?
We offer both. You can engage us for one-time projects like VAT registration or corporate tax setup, or opt for our monthly retainer plans for continuous accounting and tax support.

### 9. How do I get started with your services?
The easiest way is to book a free consultation through our website. Our experts will assess your business needs and provide a tailored proposal to get you started immediately.
